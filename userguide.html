Getting Started

When you open the application, you will be greeted by a welcome screen.


Existing Users

Tap Login and enter your registered email and password to access your dashboard.

New Users

Tap Register to create your account. You will need to provide your full name, email, a secure password, and select your designated role (Worker or Administrator).

Administrator Guide

If you are logged in as an Administrator, your dashboard serves as a central command hub for monitoring factory health and safety.


Factory Overview & Controls

Your dashboard displays summary cards giving you an instant glance at the factory's operational health. You can also monitor and toggle sewing floor device controls directly from this screen.

Attendance & Security

Tap Attendance to monitor the live camera feed at the factory entrance and view secure, blockchain-verified attendance records. Tap "Refresh" to fetch the latest data.

Safety Ledger

Access the Safety Ledger to review a highly secure, immutable list of past safety events and their verification statuses.

Emergency Response

If an incident occurs, you will receive emergency alerts in your notifications. Open the alert to review the details, dispatch necessary assistance, and mark the situation as "Resolved" once it has been handled.

PPE Verification

Open the PPE Check module to monitor the automated safety equipment checks happening at the factory entrances.

Factory Worker Guide

If you are logged in as a Worker, your dashboard is streamlined to focus on your daily tasks, equipment maintenance, and personal safety.


Daily Tasks & Profile

Check your dashboard for today's assignments. You can tap the Profile icon in the top navigation bar at any time to review your personal details or securely log out.

Health Monitoring

Tap the Health Widget to view interactive graphs of your heart rate and body temperature over time. Pay close attention to this screen—if any limits are exceeded, the system will display colour-coded warnings advising you to take action.

Augmented Reality (AR) Maintenance

To perform a maintenance check on your equipment, tap Scan Machinery (AR). Point your device’s camera at the machinery and follow the holographic text and arrows that appear on your screen for guided assistance.

Emergency Reporting (SOS)

In the event of an accident or hazard, tap the red SOS button on your dashboard. Select the type of emergency, type in any optional details, and tap Submit Alert to instantly notify the administration team.

Automated PPE Clearance

When entering the factory floor, an automated kiosk handles your safety clearance.

  1. Stand clearly in front of the entrance camera.
  2. The system will automatically scan you for required Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), such as masks and gloves.
  3. If your equipment is worn correctly, you will be granted clearance. If anything is missing, the system will display a warning telling you exactly which equipment you need to put on.

How to Run This App

Follow these instructions to start the TexyShield AI backend services and run the Android mobile application.


1. Running the AI Computer Vision Pipeline

The AI service processes camera feeds for PPE detection, facial health monitoring, and anomaly detection.

  1. Open a terminal or command prompt and navigate to the ai-service directory located inside your project folder:
    cd path/to/project/ai-service
  2. Run the automated backend script (which will automatically detect and update the BASE_URL in your Android code with your machine's local IP address):
    .\venv\Scripts\python.exe run_backend.py (Windows) or python run_backend.py (Mac/Linux).
  3. Alternatively, to run the FastAPI server manually without auto-updating the IP, use:
    .\venv\Scripts\uvicorn main:app --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8000
  4. Note on API URLs: Ensure your Android app's API base URL matches the server's address. If you use run_backend.py, it detects your IP and updates this for you (e.g., http://192.168.x.x:8000/). If you are testing on an Android Emulator manually without the script, you should set the URL to http://10.0.2.2:8000/.

2. Running the Android Application

The Android app is built using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. You can run it via Android Studio.

  1. Open Android Studio and select Open an existing project.
  2. Navigate to and select the SmartFactory directory from this repository.
  3. Wait for the Gradle sync to complete successfully. The project already includes the required google-services.json for Firebase connection.
  4. Connect a physical Android device via USB (with USB Debugging enabled) or start an Android Virtual Device (Emulator).
  5. Click the green Run (▶) button in the Android Studio toolbar to compile and install the app on your device.
